About.

Developed during a one-week workshop at the UPDATE Festival in Liepāja, this project explores the city’s former military fortifications and the layers of history, decay and contemporary art embedded within them. This website serves as a record of a place that is still changing, before it disappears.

ABANDONED AFTER LESS THAN A DECADE.

1908,The fort was closed because it was considered a strategic mistake. Some cannons were moved to Kaunas Fortress, while others were melted down. The fortifications were later demolished, but some artillery batteries, underground structures, and powder warehouses remain today..

MILITARY FORTIFICATION

1899–1908 Liepāja was developed into a fortified military port. Coastal and land fortifications were constructed to protect the naval base from potential attacks.

RUINs

From 1908 After the fortifications were abandoned in 1908, parts of the structures were demolished. However, several artillery positions, underground spaces, and former powder magazines remained and gradually became ruins..

05 / LOCATION

THE COAST TAKES OVER.

Located along the coast, the bunkers are constantly exposed to erosion, wind, and the sea. Over time, parts of them have already disappeared, and more may eventually be lost.
